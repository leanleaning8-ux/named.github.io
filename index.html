Welcome to Naira Floor, a stock-market education application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and handle information when you use our application.

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect:

Personal information is retained only for as long as needed for the purposes described in this policy or as required by applicable law. Users may request information about deletion and retention.
