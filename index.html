About our school

A home for learning, right in the heart of Umuezeagu

Divine Gift Int'l Nursery/Primary School has grown into a trusted name for parents in Umuezeagu Uruagu and the wider Nnewi community. From a child's first day in day care through to primary school, our teachers give every pupil the attention, discipline and warmth they need to grow.

Our compound is built for learning and for play — bright classrooms, a supervised gate, and staff who know every child by name. We believe a good school feels like a second home.

Our motto

What we offer

Three stages, one steady path

Each stage is built around the age and needs of the child, so learning always feels like the right next step.

Ages 6 months – 2 years

Day Care

A warm, secure space for our youngest children — supervised play, feeding, naptime and early sensory learning, in the hands of caring staff.

Ages 3 – 5 years

Nursery

Letters, numbers, songs and stories. Nursery builds the foundation — curiosity, listening, sharing, and the first steps toward reading and writing.

Ages 6 – 11 years

Primary

A full primary curriculum delivered with discipline and care, preparing pupils for secondary school with strong reading, maths and moral grounding.
